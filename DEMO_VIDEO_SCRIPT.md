# ItsEasyNow AI Recruiter — 3–5 Minute Demo Script

## 0:00–0:25 — Problem and product

Show the landing page: “ItsEasyNow AI Recruiter uses Nova to run a structured first-round screening conversation and turn it into recruiter-ready evidence.”

## 0:25–1:00 — Candidate-ready flow

Open the interview. Show microphone permission, typed-answer fallback, consent, and profile validation.

## 1:00–2:25 — Voice interview

Show Nova’s TTS greeting, voice input, timer, waveform, repeat-question action, and transcript. Use responses that mention education, React/TypeScript, a project, and career availability. Highlight the technical and project follow-ups.

## 2:25–3:20 — Recruiter decision

Complete the interview, open Recruiter Dashboard, select Review, then show transcript, score dimensions, strengths, concerns, recommendation, status update, and private notes.

## 3:20–4:00 — Operations and responsible AI

Show search, filters, CSV export, transcript download, and Analytics. Close with: “The AI provides decision support; a recruiter makes the final hiring decision.”
